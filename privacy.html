Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how Na Lupa Publisher ("the app"), a desktop tool used by the Na Lupa team to publish its own videos, handles information when an account owner connects a TikTok or YouTube account.

1. Information we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and creator information returned by the Content Posting API (such as available privacy options and maximum video length), only to show which account is connected and to offer the correct posting options.
  • YouTube / Google: permission to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel and to read the channel's basic information (channel name and ID) to confirm the correct channel is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and Google after the owner signs in.

2. How we use it

The information is used only to publish videos that the account owner chooses, with the caption and settings the owner confirms. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the computer where the app runs, in a private file readable only by the local user. The app has no server and no database. Videos and captions stay on that computer until they are sent to the platform the owner chose.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok or Google, through their official APIs, when the owner publishes a video.

5. Google API Services

Na Lupa Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

  • TikTok: open the TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, and remove Na Lupa Publisher.
  • Google: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Na Lupa Publisher.
  • Revoking access makes the stored token stop working. You can also ask us to delete the local token file at any time by email (see Contact below); we delete it within 7 days.

7. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and is used only by the Na Lupa team.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
