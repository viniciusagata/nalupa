Na Lupa Publisher

Na Lupa is a Brazilian channel of short animated videos that explain everyday curiosities (Instagram and TikTok @nalupa.br, YouTube @nalupa_br). Every video is original content written and produced by the Na Lupa team.

What this app does

Na Lupa Publisher is a desktop publishing tool used by the Na Lupa team to publish our own finished videos to our own accounts, instead of uploading each video by hand on every platform.

  • The account owner signs in with the platform's official login (TikTok Login Kit / Google OAuth) and grants permission.
  • The tool shows which account is connected and lets the owner choose the caption, title, privacy level and interaction settings before each post.
  • The video is sent through the official APIs: TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API v3.
  • Nothing is posted without the owner's explicit confirmation.

The tool runs only on the team's computer. It has no public sign-up and does not access any account other than the ones the owner connects.
